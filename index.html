How it works

Three steps, one transaction.

  1. 01Fill in the form

    Name, ticker, description and a photo. Add a dev buy or social links if you want them.

  2. 02Connect and confirm

    Connect your wallet on Base and approve one transaction. Your wallet becomes the token admin.

  3. 03Trade right away

    Clanker creates the token and its Uniswap v4 pool in the same transaction, so it can trade immediately.

FAQ

Good to know

What does it cost?

Gas on Base, plus the dev buy if you add one. Bloom charges no launch fee. Clanker keeps a share of trading fees, so check its docs for the current split.

Who controls the token?

The wallet you connect is set as the token admin. You can use it to update token details and claim rewards through Clanker's tools.

Can I undo a launch?

No. On-chain deployments are final, so double-check the name, ticker and image before you confirm.

Is this financial advice?

No. Anyone can launch a token and many go to zero. Do your own research and follow the rules where you live.

Launching tokens is risky and nothing here is financial advice. Make sure you understand the rules that apply to you before you deploy.
